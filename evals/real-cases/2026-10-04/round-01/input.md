Decision frame: 2019–2021 programme renewal; not a reenactment of its original launch.
Primary source: Bord Bia, Origin Green Report, Next Phase section, printed pp90–91 (PDF pages88–89). https://www.bordbia.ie/globalassets/origin-green/og-publications/origin-green-progress-report-update.pdf

Source-derived paraphrases:
- Established in 2012. Commissioned PwC research found Ireland's sustainable-food reputation credible but requiring demonstrable measurement.
- Renewal drew on 2018 customer/consumer, farmer, and food-company research.
- PwC studied 13 markets, interviewing senior procurement/sustainability professionals and surveying over 8,500 consumers.
- Western European trade customers were important drivers; consumer concerns outside Europe more often emphasized health and food safety.
- Consumer and trade meanings of sustainability differed. Grass-fed and information transparency emerged as opportunities; advanced buyers wanted greater detail.
