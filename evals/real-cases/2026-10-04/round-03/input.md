# Futog cabbage — disclosed evidence partition (real case)
Decision frame: build an effective protected-origin route to market after the documented 2007 specifications work.
Primary source: Jasna Mastilović and Aleksanda Novaković, Defining of the Geographic Determinants Connected with the Futog Cabbage in Serbia, FAO-hosted presentation. https://www.fao.org/fileadmin/templates/olq/documents/serbia/AleksandraNovakovic.pdf
PDF metadata title identifies Belgrade 3–4 December 2008; title slide lacks printed date. Retrieved and text verified 2026-10-04.

Source-derived paraphrases, PDF slide numbers:
- Slides3–6: farmers retained selected plants for seed; local cultivation and processing produced distinctive fresh/fermented cabbage properties.
- Slides7–8: source reports valued demand in Serbia and abroad, but both non-Futog and hybrid cabbage were being labelled Futog.
- Slides10–12: producers/processors formed an association; approached Novi Sad food-technologies institute in February2007; jointly developed specifications through December2007.
- Slides19–20,24–25: thin, flexible leaves; suitability for souring; fermented product must use native Futog cabbage.
- Slides27–30: association and quality definition were key; provincial/ministry support included specification work, equipment and marketing; stronger marketing was proposed to reward producers.

Limits: promotional/scientific presenter claims, not representative purchase evidence. No verified consumer choice experiment, channel margin, unit economics or stated willingness-to-pay in this baseline. Do not invent them.
